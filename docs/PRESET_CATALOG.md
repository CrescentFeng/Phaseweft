# Factory preset catalog — v0.7

The initial bank is deliberately small enough to audit. It covers distinct jobs instead of minor parameter variations.

## Utility

- **Gentle Width** — restrained adaptive width for inserts.
- **Mix Air Motion** — high-frequency motion at a low wet level.
- **Mono-Safe Slow** — minimal stereo phase offset for safer mono fold-down.

## Drums

- **Transient Halo** — preserves attacks while the sustain moves on a whole-note cycle.
- **Hi-Hat Orbit** — high-focused, wide rhythmic field.
- **Snare Vacuum** — negative-feedback through-zero cancellation.
- **Drum Bus Lift** — subtle transient/sustain separation for a bus.

## Guitar

- **Zero Jet** — pronounced through-zero sweep.
- **Clean Double** — restrained classic doubling/flanging.
- **Analog Sweep** — coloured, tempo-synced classic motion.

## Synth

- **Pad Drift** — slow four-bar field evolution.
- **Bass Current** — low-focused movement with limited width.
- **Arp Lattice** — rhythmic high-motion field for arpeggios.
- **Glass Orbit** — transient-aware stereo movement.

## Experimental

- **Negative Spiral** — wide negative cross-feedback pattern.
- **Field Collapse** — deep, narrow-centred adaptive field.
- **Feedback Bloom** — slow coloured through-zero feedback.
- **Broken Compass** — fast asymmetric transient motion.

Six representative renders are stored under `artifacts/audio/featured-presets/`. They use the same procedural source as the four engine comparisons and are intended for direct design evaluation, not as mastered marketing material.
